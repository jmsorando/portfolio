To Whom It May Concern:

Re: José Miguel Sorando Campillo

I am writing to provide a strong reference for José Miguel Sorando Campillo, who completed an undergraduate industry placement with ORB Innovations Ltd in our Human Performance & Health (HP&H) team from 1 September to 12 December 2025, as part of his Sport & Exercise Medical Sciences BSc at University College London.

José was consistently professional, reliable, and committed. He communicated clearly, was punctual and well prepared, and could be relied upon to follow protocols precisely—particularly important in a human performance setting involving supervised exercise testing, physiological monitoring, and work with research volunteers. He worked responsibly within the limits of his role, asked the right questions at the right time, and took feedback on board quickly and positively.

José also showed strong development in technical and analytical work. During the placement he developed practical skills in Python for data analysis, applying them in a careful and methodical way. He demonstrated an excellent appreciation for data quality and measurement considerations, and he approached analysis with the curiosity and discipline you want in someone moving toward STEM-focused postgraduate study.

A standout contribution was José’s presentation on the validity of a smart mouthguard to measure heart rate during Muay Thai training and sparring. He took a proactive approach
